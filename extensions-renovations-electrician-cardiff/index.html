Extensions & renovations

Electrics for extensions, kitchens & renovations

Complete electrical work for extensions, kitchens, bathrooms and refurbishments across Cardiff — working directly for you or as part of your builder’s team.

Request a Quote 07590 275205

What we do on renovation projects

  • Extensions and loft conversions — new circuits, sockets, lighting and heating controls
  • Kitchens — cooker and appliance circuits, island power, downlights, under-cabinet and plinth lighting
  • Bathrooms — LED mirrors, shower and feature lighting, extractor fans, electric towel rails and underfloor heating, installed to bathroom zone rules
  • Whole-house refurbishments — often combined with a rewire and consumer unit upgrade
  • Outdoor — garden, patio and step lighting and outdoor sockets

Planned around the build

On a renovation, timing matters. We do the first fix (cables and back boxes) before plasterboard and plastering, and come back for the second fix (sockets, switches and lights) once decorating is done. We’ll agree the dates with you or your builder so we’re never the trade holding the job up.

Get the layout right first

It’s much cheaper to put a socket or light in the right place at first fix than to move it later. Before we start we’ll walk through the space with you and think about:

  • Where furniture, the TV and appliances will go
  • Enough sockets, including USB sockets where useful
  • Lighting in layers — general downlights, task lighting and feature lighting
  • Two-way switching and dimmers in the right places
  • Outdoor lighting and power if the extension opens onto the garden

Certified and notified

New circuits and most renovation electrical work are notifiable under the Building Regulations. We test everything, issue an Electrical Installation Certificate and notify the work to Building Control, so your paperwork is in order for sign-off and when you come to sell.

Frequently asked questions

Can you work with my builder?

Yes. We work alongside builders, kitchen and bathroom fitters and carpenters, and plan first and second fix around their programme.

When should I book an electrician for an extension?

As early as possible — ideally when the builder is booked — so first fix can be scheduled before plasterboard goes up.

Do you handle Building Regulations for the electrics?

Yes. We test and certify the work and notify it to Building Control for you.
