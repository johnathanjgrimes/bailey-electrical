If you let a property in Wales, electrical safety is part of the **fitness for human habitation** requirements under the Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016. These have applied to new occupation contracts since **1 December 2022**, and to converted contracts since 1 December 2023.

This guide sums up what that means in practice. It isn't legal advice — check the sources at the bottom for the full detail.

## The EICR requirement

- You must have a **valid Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR)** for the property during each occupation contract.
- A report lasts a **maximum of five years** from the date of inspection — or less, if the electrician recommends an earlier re-test on the report.
- The contract-holder must be given a copy. Shelter Cymru advises this should be **within 14 days** of the occupation contract starting.

If you're not complying with the fitness requirements, the Welsh Government says you **can't issue a landlord's notice or use a break clause**.

> Many online guides quote the **England** rules (the 2020 Electrical Safety Standards regulations). Wales has its own rules — make sure you're following the right ones.

## Smoke and carbon monoxide alarms

The same fitness requirements cover alarms:

- **Smoke alarms** on every storey, **connected to the mains** and **interlinked** with the other mains alarms. Extra battery alarms are allowed on top.
- **Carbon monoxide alarms** in every room with a gas, oil or solid fuel appliance.

We can install mains interlinked smoke and heat alarms at the same time as the EICR or any remedial work.

## What the EICR codes mean

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **C1** | Danger present | Immediate action needed |
| **C2** | Potentially dangerous | Urgent remedial work needed |
| **FI** | Further investigation needed | Investigate without delay |
| **C3** | Improvement recommended | Not a fail — worth considering |

Any **C1, C2 or FI** means the report is **unsatisfactory** and the work needs putting right. Once it's done, you'll get written confirmation that the issues have been fixed.

## Appliances (PAT testing)

If the property is let furnished with electrical appliances, Shelter Cymru says these should also be PAT tested.
