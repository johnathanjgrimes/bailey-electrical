A new open-plan kitchen needs its electrics planned before the units go in. On this job we installed:

- **Recessed downlights** across the kitchen and dining area for even, glare-free general lighting
- **Under-cabinet LED strip** lighting the worktops — the task lighting that makes a kitchen usable
- **Power for the island**, so it works as a proper prep and seating area
- **Appliance circuits** for the ovens, hob and integrated appliances

Getting the layout right at first fix — before plastering — is what makes a kitchen like this look clean, with no visible cables and switches exactly where they're needed.
