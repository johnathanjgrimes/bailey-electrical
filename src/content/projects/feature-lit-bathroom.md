Bathrooms have the strictest wiring rules in the house. Everything is installed according to the **bathroom zones** in the wiring regulations, with the right IP-rated fittings and RCD protection.

On this bathroom we fitted:

- A **backlit LED mirror** as the main feature
- **Recessed shower lighting** rated for use in the shower zone
- **LED plinth lighting** under the vanity unit for a soft night-light effect
- Power for a **heated towel rail**
