Good garden lighting turns a patio into somewhere you'll actually use in the evening. On this garden we installed:

- **Step lights** so the steps are safe after dark
- **Fence and wall lights** along the patio
- **Uplighting** on the trees and planting for depth

Outdoor circuits need weatherproof fittings, suitable cable and RCD protection. If you're having a new patio or landscaping done, it's easiest to run the cables before the paving goes down.
